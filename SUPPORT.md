# Support

Get help with My Journal, report a problem or suggest an improvement. Questions are welcome too.

**Keep private details out.** Issues are public. Never include journal text or titles, passwords, recovery keys, setup or recovery codes, agent connection files or tokens in an issue or email. Use made-up examples instead, and check logs and screenshots before you attach them.

## Find an answer

- [User guide](docs/guide/README.md): getting started, writing and organizing, sync, devices, backups, App Lock and agent access.
- [Troubleshooting](docs/guide/troubleshooting.md): a forgotten master password, unlocking, changes that need review, sync problems, moving to a new device, and erasing or deleting your data.

## Ask a question or report a problem

[Open an issue](https://github.com/ralphkrauss/my-journal/issues/new/choose) on GitHub. Choose Bug report for something that doesn’t work and Feature request for an idea; for a question, choose the one that fits best. [Search the existing issues](https://github.com/ralphkrauss/my-journal/issues) first; someone may have asked already.

Include the app version, your device and system version, and the steps that lead to the problem. To find the app version:

- Mac: My Journal > About My Journal.
- iPhone and iPad: in My Journal, Settings, at the end of the About section.

No GitHub account? Email [ralph@krauss.be](mailto:ralph@krauss.be).

## Report a security problem

Report security problems privately, not in a public issue: open the repository’s Security tab and choose [Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/ralphkrauss/my-journal/security/advisories/new). The [security model](SECURITY.md) explains what is protected.

## Keep your master password safe

Keep your master password in a password manager. Without it, you can’t open an encrypted archive or recover your journals if you lose all your devices.

## Privacy

My Journal doesn’t collect personal data. See the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md).
