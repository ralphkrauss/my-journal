Learning to rest

By Sam Lee · 4 min read

Most of us treat rest as a reward. It works better as a habit. Here is what helped me, with notes from a 2024 study.

What changed

Short walks after lunch
No email after 7 pm
Phone in another room
Reading on paper
Pick one habit
Keep it for two weeks
Rest is not idleness.
