# Sample photo prompts

The five sample photos were made on 2026-09-28 with Codex's image generation (`codex exec` asked to use its image generation tool; one PNG per file, 1448 × 1086), then cropped and saved as JPEG by `design/app-store/make_photos.py`. Each prompt below is the shared style followed by the subject. Every image was checked at full size; the first `loaf` (a bent rack) and `path` (a mesh texture over the leaves) were generated again with the second style and the subjects shown here.

## Style

First run (`coffee`, `tomatoes`, `river`):

> A natural, believable photograph taken with a good camera by an ordinary person for their own journal. Warm, soft, natural light, realistic colors, gentle depth of field, fine natural detail and texture, slight real-world imperfection. No people, no faces, no hands, no text or lettering anywhere, no logos, no brands, no labels, no watermarks, no borders. It must not look like an illustration, a 3D render or an advertisement. Landscape orientation, 4:3.

Second run (`loaf`, `path`):

> A natural, believable photograph taken with a good camera by an ordinary person for their own journal. Warm, soft, natural light, realistic colors, gentle depth of field, fine natural detail, clean sharp focus without any repeating pattern, grid, mesh or screen-door texture over the image, no noise pattern. No people, no faces, no hands, no text or lettering anywhere, no logos, no brands, no labels, no watermarks, no borders. It must not look like an illustration, a 3D render or an advertisement. Landscape orientation, 4:3.

## Subjects

- **coffee.jpg:** A white ceramic cup of black coffee on a saucer, on a worn wooden kitchen table beside a sunny window on a Sunday morning. Soft sunlight falls across the table in window-shaped patches. An open plain notebook with blank pages and a pencil lies nearby, partly in frame. Seen from slightly above.
- **tomatoes.jpg:** A simple ceramic bowl of freshly picked garden tomatoes, mostly ripe red with a few green and one orange, some with bits of vine and green stems, sitting on a painted white wooden windowsill in soft evening light. A few basil leaves beside the bowl.
- **loaf.jpg:** A homemade round sourdough loaf with a clearly raised ear along its score and a light dusting of flour, cooling on a simple rectangular stainless steel wire cooling rack with evenly spaced straight parallel wires, the whole rack visible and resting flat on a folded linen kitchen towel on a kitchen counter, soft daylight from a window to the side.
- **path.jpg:** A narrow forest path covered in wet orange, red and brown fallen autumn leaves on a rainy morning, winding away between tall tree trunks, soft mist in the distance, damp and quiet. Each leaf looks individual and natural, some glistening with raindrops. Eye-level view. No people.
- **river.jpg:** A riverside old town at sunset: colorful narrow houses with tiled facades and terracotta roofs stacked up a hillside along a stone quay, several small traditional wooden boats moored on the calm river in the foreground, warm golden light and soft reflections on the water, seen from the opposite bank. No people visible, no readable signs.
