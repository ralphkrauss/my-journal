# My Journal user guide

My Journal is a private journal with a clean, simple interface that feels at home on your device. Your journals stay on your devices, encrypted, and sync only through a server you run, or not at all.

The first apps are for iPhone, iPad and Mac. This guide uses the names of menus and buttons as they appear there, and says where the Mac differs from iPhone and iPad.

## Topics

- [Getting started](getting-started.md): install the app, create your journal, choose a master password, write, organize, and update.
- [Sync](sync.md): keep your journals the same on all your devices, through a server you run.
- [Devices](devices.md): add a device, compare the check code, and remove a device.
- [Backups](backups.md): export an archive, restore or import one, and export your journals as Markdown.
- [App Lock](app-lock.md): require Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode to open the app.
- [Agent access](agent-access.md): let an AI agent read the journals you choose.
- [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md): a forgotten password, unlocking, changes that need review, sync problems, moving to a new device, and deleting your data.

## Where to find Settings

- **Mac:** My Journal > Settings… (⌘,). The tabs are General, Sync (which lists your devices), Privacy, Backup and Agent Access. **Erase Journals and Settings…** is at the end of General.
- **iPhone and iPad:** tap Settings (the gear) in Journals. The sections are General, Sync (which lists your devices), Privacy, Backup and Agent Access, with **Erase Journals and Settings…** at the end.
